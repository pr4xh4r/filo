# Filo Complete Brand & Illustration Identity

> **Art Direction**: Minimalist Scandinavian Editorial Monoline Vector Art with Translucent Frosted Glass Geometry  
> **Aesthetic Reference**: Smooth, continuous single-weight black line art (`#111827`) paired with soft translucent pastel glass accents.  
> **Color Harmony**: **Ethereal Dawn** (Warm apricot rose `#FFD7CA`, Champagne glow `#FFF2EB`, Misty arctic lavender `#E3DCF7`, and translucent frosted glass refractions).
